About

SEO audits for websites that render in the browser.

CrawlLoom is a browser-based crawler built to inspect modern websites, including pages whose meaningful content appears only after JavaScript runs.

What it does

CrawlLoom renders a target page in Chromium where available, then extracts page text, metadata, headings, links, status information, and content-area findings. It can also fall back to direct HTML extraction where browser rendering is unavailable.

Designed for content audits

The crawler is especially useful for checking dynamically generated editorial content, including content published through CMS platforms such as Kentico. Its content-area detection supports known selectors and a text-focused heuristic for pages with differing layouts.

How results are handled

Each browser tab has its own crawl session. Completed crawl history can be stored in the deployment's configured MySQL database, exported as CSV or Excel, and managed by an authorised administrator.
